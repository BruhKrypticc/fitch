HOW IT WORKS

The process is simple:

  1. Create your school

    Sign in as an administrator, give your school a name, and get a five-character join code to share with students.

  2. Share the code

    Students join with a school code and choose their own display name. Organizers can optionally require a verified school Google account, which assigns a generated alias instead.

  3. Keep everyone in the loop

    Post updates, link your calendar, and answer questions in one place.

Finch Update · Now live

Meet Finch, your school assistant.

Ask about bell schedules, handbooks, policies, announcements, and photos. Finch answers only from your school’s own information, and says so plainly when it doesn’t know. Test out Finch by creating a school, adding context and asking as a student!

  • Grounded in correct and up-to-date school information
  • Tap the Finch bird at the bottom-right of any school page
  • Organizers control it from Finch Settings
  • Finch screens polls, questions, replies and announcements to keep the board school-appropriate
