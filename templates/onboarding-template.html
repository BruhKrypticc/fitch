Getting started

  1. Open Fitch and choose Join a school.
  2. Enter the school code {{schoolCode}}.
  3. Pick a display name (please don’t use a real name) and start reading the board.

Install Fitch on your phone or computer

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Fitch in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Android and desktop (Chrome)

  1. Open Fitch in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots), then choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On a computer, click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Confirm by selecting Install.

What you can do with Fitch

Document Locker

Keep handbooks, forms, and permission slips in one searchable place that students and families can open any time.

Class office polls

Organizers and class officers can publish polls, and results update live. Only organizers can delete a poll.

Finch AI assistant

Finch answers questions from your school’s own information and says so plainly when it doesn’t know.

Every poll, question, reply, and announcement passes through free automatic content moderation before it is posted, which helps keep Fitch school-appropriate at no cost to your school.

Tips for organizers

  • Add class officers from Settings so they can help answer questions and run polls.
  • Fill the Finch knowledge base with bell schedules, policies, and handbooks.
  • Never upload private student information. Files can be opened by anyone with the link.
