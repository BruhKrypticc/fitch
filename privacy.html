Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Fitch is a school communication tool for announcements, a school calendar, questions and answers, photos, class office polls, a document locker, and Finch, an AI school assistant. This policy explains what information Fitch handles and how it is used. We designed Fitch to collect as little as possible, especially from students.

Who this applies to

  • Students join a school with a school code. They do not need to create a Fitch account.
  • Organizers and class officers sign in with a Google account to manage a school’s content.

Information we handle

Students. By default, a student picks a display name. We ask students not to use their real name. If a school turns on Safe Mode (required school Google sign-in), students sign in with a verified school Google account and are given a generated display name instead. In that case we use the account only to verify that the student belongs to the school and to keep one display name per account. Other students and staff only see the display name.

Organizers and class officers. We receive the basic Google account details needed to sign in, such as your email address and a unique account ID. Officers are invited by email, and their posts are labeled “Class officer” rather than with their name.

Content people submit. This includes announcements, questions, answers, poll questions and votes, photos and captions, uploaded documents, and any information an organizer adds to the Finch knowledge base. Please do not post private information about yourself or others.

Poll votes. Votes are anonymous to other users. Without Safe Mode, a random ID stored in your browser limits each device to one vote. With Safe Mode, one vote is allowed per verified school account.

On your device. Fitch stores a few items in your browser’s local storage to work properly, such as the school you joined, your display name, and a random voting ID. Clearing your browser data removes them.

How we use information

  • To run the features above for the school you belong to.
  • To show content only within the school it was posted to.
  • To keep content appropriate for a school community (see “Content moderation”).
  • To answer questions through Finch using your school’s own information.

We do not sell personal information, we do not show advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles of students.

Content moderation

Before a poll, question, answer, announcement, or photo caption is saved, the text is sent to the OpenAI moderation service to check whether it is harmful or inappropriate. Content that is flagged is not posted, and the author is asked to reword it. We send only the text being checked, not names or account details.

Finch, the AI assistant

When you ask Finch a question, your question and relevant excerpts from your school’s announcements, knowledge base, and photo captions are sent to Google’s Gemini service to generate an answer. Finch is designed to answer only from your school’s information. AI answers can be wrong, so check important details with your school office. Please do not enter private information into Finch.

Translation

If you choose another language, the text being translated is sent to the MyMemory translation service. Only the text is sent, not your name or account.

Services that help us run Fitch

Fitch relies on the following providers, which process information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, and hosting) stores accounts and school content.
  • ImageKit stores uploaded photos and documents.
  • Cloudflare runs the secure relays that connect Fitch to the AI and moderation services.
  • Google Gemini powers Finch.
  • OpenAI provides content moderation.
  • MyMemory provides translation.
  • Google Calendar displays a school calendar when an organizer links one.

Photos and documents are reachable by anyone who has the file link, so organizers should never upload private student information.

Children’s privacy

Fitch is designed to align with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly ask children under 13 for their real name, contact details, or other personal information. Students use a display name, or a generated one in Safe Mode. Schools decide whether to use Fitch with their students and are responsible for any consent their school requires. If you believe a child has shared personal information through Fitch, contact us and we will remove it.

Who can see what

Content posted to a school is visible to people who join that school with its code. Organizers can remove announcements, questions, answers, photos, documents, and polls. Only organizers can delete polls.

Keeping and deleting information

School content stays until it is deleted by an organizer or officer, or until the school is removed. To ask us to delete information, including a display name or a post, contact us using the details below.

Security

We use industry-standard services and access rules so that only authorized organizers can manage a school, and our AI and moderation keys are kept on the server rather than in the app. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please share only what you are comfortable sharing with your school community.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Fitch changes. We will change the “Last updated” date above when we do.
